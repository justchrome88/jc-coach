# Master Work-Package Checklist

Source preserved at
`_legacy_archive/r02a2-2026-07-11/docs/project_management/MASTER_WP_CHECKLIST.md`.

- [ ] Explicit task scope and authority identified.
- [ ] Product and PM repositories clean on the required branch before work.
- [ ] Current/next route agrees across Product and PM human/machine state.
- [ ] Risky DB/import/parser/model/service/package actions explicitly authorized.
- [ ] Required backups, hashes, rollback points, and fixed entrypoints captured.
- [ ] Focused tests and full required quality gate pass.
- [ ] Status, handoff, registry, and relevant checklists updated together.
- [ ] Historical evidence preserved; no blocker silently closed.
- [ ] `git diff --check` and final status reviewed.
- [ ] Local commits follow task/mode authority; no push without explicit request.
