# Aim and Weapon Contract

Weapon identity must normalize parser aliases before joining fire, hit, damage, kill, and headshot events. Accuracy is accepted hits divided by accepted shots for the same normalized weapon. It is not damage accuracy or headshot-kill rate.

Match 124 stores separate `ak47` and `weapon_ak47` buckets, so current accuracy is disputed. Weapon HS% is headshot kills divided by weapon kills. Crosshair placement, first-bullet accuracy, spray control, and TTK remain unavailable without the required trajectory/view-angle/timeline evidence.
